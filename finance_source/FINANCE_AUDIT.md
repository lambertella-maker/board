# Finance audit — 22 September 2026

This is the plain-English audit of the active dashboard model. The source of truth is
`finance_inputs.json`; `finance_model.py` derives repeated values; the three dashboard
templates are generated by `build_finance.py`.

## Why the liquid figure appeared to drop

The drop was a definition and coverage problem, not evidence of money disappearing.
The net-worth widget counted UK savings, taxable US brokerage, and US cash as liquid,
but omitted the recorded Fidelity Advisor Growth Opps Class C holding (`advisorBalanceUsd`)
from both the current net-worth segments and the liquid subtotal. The dated
`net_worth.md` snapshot also uses older balances, including a lower Move Pot figure and
older UK totals. The active model now counts the Advisor holding as `US Advisory` in
total net worth and liquid assets, while continuing to keep Roth, pension, and locked SIP
shares visible but outside the liquid subtotal.

## Documents reviewed

- `finance_inputs.json` — canonical raw inputs and policy flags.
- `finance_model.py` and `finance_runtime.js` — derived and date-sensitive calculations.
- `templates/dashboard.html`, `templates/dashboard_ipad.html`, and
  `templates/dashboard_iphone.html` — active surfaces.

  not used by the dashboard renderer.

  older balances.
- `CLAUDE_INDEX.md`, `README.md`, `finances_index.py`, and `chatbot_server.py` — path

Historical notes, statements, payslips, tax records, travel records, and archived
dashboards were left in place because they are source evidence or separately linked
projects. No folder was moved, so hard-coded paths and LaunchAgents remain valid.
